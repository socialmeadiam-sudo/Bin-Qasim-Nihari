Our Story

Old Karachi flavour, still cooked the slow way.

Bin Qasim Nihari is a neighbourhood Nihari house at Plot 63 L, Block 6 P.E.C.H.S. — serving a proper bowl of traditional Nihari the way it should be: slow-cooked for hours, spiced generously, and finished fresh for every customer.

Our recipe comes from generations of Karachi Nihari cooking. We use whole spices, tender cuts of meat, and a patient simmer that turns a simple bowl of stew into something worth planning your day around.
